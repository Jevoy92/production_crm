## Hook patterns (still useful as OPEN-movement examples)

Six proven openings, with the creator who proved them. Pick or remix when designing the OPEN of any script:

1. **The Quiet Universal** (Johnny Harris) — "Year after year, more of us are quietly asking the internet, why am I so tired?" A felt experience framed as a shared secret. Strong fit for Jevoy Palmer.
2. **The Contrarian Swap** (Leila Hormozi) — "What if I told you confidence has nothing to do with feeling ready and everything to do with doing it scared?" Take the assumed cause, replace with the real one, in one sentence. Strong fit for Palmer House.
3. **The Stat-Empathy Open** (Ali Abdaal) — "Adults in the US feel tired three days a week… but the problem isn't your sleep — it's the anxiety *about* your sleep." Number, then twist.
4. **The Impossible Biography** (Veritasium) — "He might be the most important physicist ever. He never won a Nobel Prize." Paradox in two sentences; demands resolution. Fits Case File format.
5. **The Time Machine** (Dan Martell) — "I'm 44. If I could go back…" Authority via hindsight; **use carefully at launch stage** — see Launch-Stage Honesty in v3 Constitution.
6. **The Dedication** (Matt Gray) — "To everyone who's ever been told they can't — this video is for you." Names the tribe before the topic. Fits Letter format.

## Language & delivery patterns (still load-bearing)

These survive into v3's Kitchen-Table Voice rule — kept here because the examples are useful:

- **Short declarative bursts after long builds.** Harris: "Make a bunch of decisions, you feel tired even if you didn't lift a finger." Earn the punch with a wind-up.
- **Second person dominates.** Count the "you"s in any Hormozi script — they outnumber "I" 3-to-1 except inside personal stories.
- **Name the thing memorably.** "The founder's freeze." "Evolutionary mismatch." "Coffee naps." A named concept is a shareable concept — and a clip title.
- **Numbers with texture, not round numbers.** 35,000 decisions. 374,142 bunkers. $5,000 down to three months of runway. 13,800x.
- **Self-aware humor as pressure release.** One laugh per movement keeps gravity from becoming heaviness. v3 requires 2–3 minimum per script.
- **The 1-to-12 clip map** (Jevoy's own system): every script ships with 8–12 standalone 30–60s segments. Now codified as a v3 QA item.
- **Analogies carry the science; the science earns the analogy.** Real mechanism stated first, analogy after. Cite the real source on screen. Owning the science builds trust; renting it builds nothing.
