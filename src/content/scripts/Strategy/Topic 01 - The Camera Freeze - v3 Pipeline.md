## GATE 0 — TOPIC BRIEF

**TOPIC.** Why people get awkward the moment a camera points at them.

**WHY IT'S INTERESTING.** Universal, felt this week by almost everyone, and the real explanation is ancient machinery — not a personality flaw. The mechanism that keeps a human alive in a tribe is the same one that breaks when a lens turns on. That genuinely surprises people.

**THE EVIDENCE.**
- *Hero piece.* **Bateson, Nettle & Roberts (2006, *Biology Letters*).** Posting a picture of eyes above an honesty box ≈ tripled the payments versus a flower control. The coffee-tin study. Replications mixed — say so.
- **Duval & Wicklund (1972).** Objective self-awareness theory — being observed splits you into actor and monitor. The actor was doing fine until the monitor showed up.
- **Zajonc (1965, *Science*).** Social facilitation. Audiences *improve* simple, practiced tasks and *wreck* complex or unfamiliar ones. Arms the skeptic — "but I'm fine on stage" — and then resolves it.
- **Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000).** Spotlight effect. We overestimate observers by about 2x. Barry Manilow shirt.
- **Holzman & Rousey (1966).** Voice confrontation. Bone conduction makes your recorded voice sound wrong only to you.
- **Bailenson (Stanford, 2021).** Zoom self-view fatigue. A mirror in the corner of every conversation taxes the same machinery.
- **Hawthorne fade (Western Electric, 1924–1932).** Observation effects fade with familiarity. Original data debated — say so. Backs "record until it's boring."

**REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES.** Zoom self-view fatigue (universal, dated, named). Karaoke — the car-version-of-Whitney versus the stage-version-of-a-hostage-situation. Wedding toasts — the guy who's funny at every dinner table going flat the second a microphone arrives. Athletes who choke on camera while performing live in front of 80,000 people (Donny Anderson's free throw, Greg Norman's '96 Masters).

**THE THREE ANGLES.**
- **JEVOY PALMER — the human mechanism.** "A photo of eyes made strangers pay 3x more. That's why you freeze on camera." The ancient alarm meets the lens.
- **PALMER HOUSE — the business cost / opportunity.** "You're great in meetings and terrible on camera. It's not courage. It's a production problem with a fix."
- **MINDYOURBIZNIZ — the inner life.** "Why being seen feels like danger." The watched self vs. the unguarded self. Who gets to meet the real you.

**FORMAT CALLS.**
- JP → **The Experiment.** Jevoy tests the freeze on himself, on camera, live. Demonstrates the mechanism instead of describing it.
- PH → **Default spine.** OPEN → INVESTIGATE → TURN → LAND. Business cost demands a steady, surveyed walk through the cost-and-fix.
- MYB → **The Letter.** Direct address, start to finish, to one specific person — the founder who deleted last night's take.

**VERSES (one per venture, theme-matched, no repeats).**
- JP — **Psalm 139:14.** *I am fearfully and wonderfully made.* Fully seen, called wonderful.
- PH — **Proverbs 18:16.** *A man's gift maketh room for him.* The gift can't make room from inside a hard drive.
- MYB — **1 Samuel 16:7.** *Man looketh on the outward appearance, but the Lord looketh on the heart.* The thing that recorded you didn't see you.

**CTAs.**
- JP: locked primary — *"If this question lives in you too, stay close."* Secondary (optional, set here): **The Next Step** Friday live Q&A — "we're literally talking about this on Friday."
- PH: locked primary — *"Book a strategy call. It's a real conversation, not a sales call dressed up as one."* No secondary this topic.
- MYB: locked primary — *"Send this to the one person who needs it."* Secondary: one journaling prompt — "what would you say on camera if no one was going to watch it?"

**CARE NOTES.** None. Light topic — the worst-case anecdote (selfie deaths) is deliberately *not* used here; the Recorded Animal series will handle that one with the reverence it requires.
