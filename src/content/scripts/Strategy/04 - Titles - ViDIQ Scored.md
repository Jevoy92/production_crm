## Packaging rules applied
- Browse > search for this genre — titles sell curiosity, not keywords.
- Title names the tension; thumbnail shows the felt moment (eyes prop / deleted take / the mug and mic). Never repeat one in the other.
- Every title is a promise the script over-delivers on by minute two.
