## Content Rules (Critical)

1. **Standalone Rule** — Every script written for a stranger who has never seen anything else. Zero callbacks ("as we covered," "this week's episode," "our earlier investigation"). Test: would a first-time viewer feel they walked in late?
2. **No fabrication, ever** — No invented DMs, relatives, conversations, habits, or recent events. Honest toolkit: real verifiable stories · explicitly hypothetical ("picture this") · second-person scenes · honest composites ("a conversation I keep having") · the recording moment itself · Jevoy's real standing facts.
3. **Launch-stage honesty** — Build authority, don't claim it. "Here's what I'm building/learning/found." No inflated client counts, no soft numbers.
4. **Storytelling first** — Every piece tells a story. No listicles, no lectures.
5. **Faith capstone** — Verse last, theme-matched, quiet, never preachy. Different verse for each of the three scripts in a topic.
6. **beSettld is NOT real estate** — Luxury in-home concierge. No "first-time buyer" content.
7. **Make him want to film** — Scripts should feel like something he'd actually say.
8. **Cross-venture isolation** — Each venture has its own audience and voice. The ventures discover each other through the person, not through the content.
9. **Props that argue** — Every prop reveals, simplifies, contrasts, proves, or anchors. Never decorate.
10. **The skeptic gets dignity** — Strongest counterargument, armed with real evidence, conceded where right.
11. **Science owned** — Named researcher, year, real finding. Debates acknowledged. Unverifiable = cut.
12. **Wonder Rule** — The turn lands in play, wonder, or creative power — never just danger management.
